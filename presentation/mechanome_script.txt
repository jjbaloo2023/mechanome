SLIDE 1 · IAV movie (0:00-0:16) · CONTEXT
This is an influenza A virus being pulled into a cell. Green is epsin, red is
clathrin, magenta is the virus - you can watch the membrane-bending machinery
arrive, right on schedule. But watching WHO shows up doesn't tell you WHICH
event actually commits the pit to close.

SLIDE 2 · Biophysics problem (0:16-0:30)
That's because the decisive quantity is mechanical force - and force can't be
read from the motion of a pit. Viral entry is really a biophysics problem: we
need a model that says which protein players, and which specific domains, do
the bending.

SLIDE 3 · Mechanome engine (0:30-0:46)
So we built one. Mechanome predicts membrane mechanics from a protein's
structure - forward - and validates that prediction in reverse against real
super-resolution shape data. And it refuses to report a force wherever the
data can't constrain one.

SLIDE 4 · Conjunction ladder (0:46-1:04) · MODEL PREDICTION
Here's the central prediction. Build a pit up one factor at a time. The
crowding adaptor alone stays flat. Add the coat, add actin force - still flat.
Add the curvature-driving partner - only a dome. The pit crosses the scission
threshold, 0.030, ONLY when the crowding partner and elevated actin force are
present together. Neither alone is enough.

SLIDE 5 · analyze() demo (1:04-1:26) · LIVE
Now the part that makes it trustworthy. Same movie, same engine. With the actin
channel present, it commits to a number. Take that channel away, and force
becomes mathematically degenerate with curvature - so it refuses. No number.

SLIDE 6 · Two verdicts (1:26-1:40)
Forty-one piconewtons, identified - against a true value of forty. Versus
UNDETERMINED, where instead of guessing, it hands you the exact experiment that
would resolve it. It reports a force only when the data can bear one.

SLIDE 7 · Three-cell-line validation (1:40-2:00) · VALIDATED
And it's validated. The predicted curvature program reproduces across three
different cell lines, with the curvature scale confined to a narrow band.
Separately, the force engine recovers real measured tether forces to within
four percent.

SLIDE 8 · Orchestration / chimera (2:00-2:20) · VALIDATED
Which brings us back to the virus. Influenza needs the curvature-DRIVER, epsin
- not the size-setter. Delete epsin's curvature domain and entry drops while
bulk uptake is untouched. And grafting that one wedge onto a stalled adaptor
rescues commitment - the domain is the switch.

SLIDE 9 · GitHub (2:20-2:32)
All of this is open. The full engine, the manuscript, and every validation are
public and reproducible from source.

SLIDE 10 · Summary (2:32-2:46)
So: mechanical roles read from structure, a commitment threshold two inputs
must jointly clear, and predictions that hold up - including which adaptor a
virus co-opts.

SLIDE 11 · Agentic close (2:46-3:00)
And because it takes a movie and a plain-language question, you can hand it to
Claude - and close the loop on mechanobiology problems end to end: an answer
when the data allows, or the next experiment when it doesn't.
