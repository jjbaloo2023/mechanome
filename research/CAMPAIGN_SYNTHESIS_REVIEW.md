## Defensible synthesis

- **Public data:** The strongest completed empirical result is prediction of deposited, static S-BIAD566 cap-fit geometry. The within-cell-line grouped comparison favors the flexible curve on average; leave-whole-cell-line-out transfer is less uniform: constant area beats flexible for held-out U2OS, and the include-flagged equal-line summary favors area. These are descriptive point estimates without per-site fit covariance, calibrated uncertainty, independent culture replication, single-pit trajectories or mechanism discrimination.
- **Model-conditional mechanics:** The exact cap and membrane results establish compensation, rank and source ambiguities under their declared models. The nonlinear compact-source uniqueness and near-flat stability results require their stated stationary graph, origin/reservoir, support and full-profile controls. Unknown balanced load defeats a uniform near-flat Lipschitz inverse; a fixed *pointwise known* spatial load permits the reviewed bound on compatible complete stationary profiles. Neither a zero net force nor a perturbation label supplies that known field. No assessed public table meets the profile/load/rigidity/uncertainty contract.
- **Observation and software:** DASC's fixed downstream scoring rules do not independently identify missed structures. Shape2Fate and Myo1E/yeast checks established bounded table or metadata contracts, not a calibrated physical endpoint. The synthetic dimming example shows why full-cohort and eligible-only coverage differ in its stipulated noise model, but its 282 tracker calls exceeded the 150-call registration and it is no biological reanalysis. The repaired tracking validator separates those populations and passed its targeted tests; this is software correctness, not detector calibration.
- **Stopped and negative outcomes:** The 26-solve final nonlinear run failed its *physical residual acceptance gate*, so it yields no finite-amplitude K/K3 check and does not refute the analytic theorem. The Bucher workbook count mapping remains unresolved, with no phenotype estimate. The Myo1E positive/negative denominator, pairing and clustering and the yeast Figure 6 coordinate reference remain unverified; an access/definition gap is not an absence of relevant biology or data elsewhere. Akamatsu Figure 7 supplies a load-conditioned simulation pattern across displacement and network summaries against a constructed constant-work reference, not an experimentally verified paired high-load readout or a general refutation of prescribed-force models.

## Challenge to the proposed next step

The proposed matched-load, same-pit internalization plus spatial-network observation is a coherent **prospective measurement specification**, not a currently executable public-data analysis. Its fixed-work comparator is defined against independently calibrated resistance `k`, with `x(k)=x0*sqrt(k0/k)` once `x0,k0` and the load-to-resistance mapping are known. One low-load displacement does not itself calibrate `k`. A localized fluorescence proxy must have its own validated measurement link; absence of its increase alone cannot falsify simulated 7.5-nm barbed ends or bending energy. Event age/duration, stage, selection, imaging response, cell/preparation nesting and an independent displacement reference must be controlled to make a joint contrast interpretable. The same joint-measurement prerequisite was already stated in the reviewed actin source decision. The initial proposal suggested repeating the stopped `ACTIN_CODE_FINDINGS.md` repository inventory. The revised [proposal](CAMPAIGN_NEXT_EXPERIMENT.md) removes that executable step, fixes the resistance and proxy claims, and explicitly ends at the missing-data prerequisite. I accept this revised stop decision.

An independently assayed membrane-accessibility or internalization endpoint measured on the *same events* as clathrin tracks could answer a distinct observation question: whether track disappearance predicts that assay-defined outcome. This would not require a spatial force map, but it would require validated endpoint sensitivity and specificity, a declared lag/censoring and association rule, full-cohort selection accounting, and cell/preparation grouping. A dynamin flash or track disappearance itself is not an independent scission label. No such paired dataset has been verified in this synthesis; a later narrowly registered source screen would be feasibility work, not an empirical endpoint result.
