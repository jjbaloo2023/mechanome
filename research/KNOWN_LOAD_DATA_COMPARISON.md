# Known load is a control assumption, not a dataset label

Task known-load-recovery-001, 29 September 2026. This comparison uses the campaign's already reviewed public-data contracts; it is not a new literature search or claim that relevant observations exist nowhere. The mathematical result remains subject to the separate theory and independent review.

The comparison asks whether fixing the spatial vertical load can improve curvature-source recovery from a complete stationary graph. Even if the theorem succeeds, applying it requires all of the relevant controls together. Zero net force does not mean zero load at each radius. An actin-inhibition label does not by itself measure the residual load or balancing reaction.

| Required input or assumption | What the retained public evidence supplies | Current consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Complete normalized-slope field on a known spatial scale, with the derivatives required by the theorem | S-BIAD566 gives joint spherical-cap fits, not the complete pit and surrounding membrane profile or independent derivative-error bounds | Fitted radius/angle cannot be substituted for the theorem's profile norm |
| A single-valued regular stationary graph over the source region and a specified reservoir | 58.28% of the unflagged corrected LocMoFit cohort overhang the graph representation; stationarity and the surrounding reservoir are not measured by those tables | The theorem does not cover the full cohort; selecting shallow caps alone does not restore the other controls |
| Known spatial projected-area vertical load, including its balancing reaction; zero load is a special assumption | Bucher morphology and osmotic intervention do not calibrate spatial traction; Saleem tube-force resultant is not a measured pit-load field | Neither perturbation labels nor net/resultant force specify the load required for source recovery |
| Known rigidity and tension, and declared source support/coordinates | Reviewed Bucher controls do not provide the needed local mechanical calibration; Saleem provides calibration precedents but changes in membrane composition can alter binding | A theoretical known-control comparison is not an established intervention with invariant sources |
| Calibrated profile uncertainty and independent sampling | LocMoFit processed tables lack per-site localization inputs and fit covariance; static file/cell groups do not establish single-pit trajectories | A functional Lipschitz bound is not a calibrated likelihood or imaging-precision threshold |
| Direct evidence for membrane shape rather than fluorescence classes alone | DASC/AP2 and Shape2Fate supply narrower movie/track/metadata contracts; their observation and selection limitations remain | These cannot be promoted to complete mechanical profiles by changing labels |

Evidence: [LocMoFit observation contract](LOCMOFIT_OBSERVATION_CONTRACT.md), [independent experimental controls](INDEPENDENT_CONTROLS_FINDINGS.md), [DASC observation decision](DASC_OBSERVATION_DECISION.md), [tracking audit](ANNOTATION_AUDIT.md), and [prior source-separation findings](LOAD_IDENTIFIABILITY_FINDINGS.md). The original source locators and access limits remain in those records.

## Decision

No currently assessed dataset is promoted to a calibrated source-recovery analysis by this comparison. Preserve the descriptive geometry result and existing data contracts. Do not rerun geometry rankings, reopen Bucher count reconciliation, download movies, or treat a stronger mathematical theorem as permission to fit missing controls.

After the fixed-load theorem is reviewed, close this sequence of unrestricted source-recovery tests with a single control/observation decision. Any further empirical branch should name a concrete prediction, the actual measured quantity, independent control evidence, sampling unit and a finite acquisition budget before code or data retrieval. The campaign should not fill future cycles with progressively narrower theorem variations or repeated audits of the same missing information.
