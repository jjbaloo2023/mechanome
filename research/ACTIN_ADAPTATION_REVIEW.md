Task `actin-adaptation-review-001`, 29 September 2026. I checked the [registered design](actin_adaptation_prediction_design.json), the corrected [source note](ACTIN_ADAPTATION_SOURCE.md), indexed primary text for [Akamatsu et al. 2020](https://elifesciences.org/articles/49840) and its [PMC record](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7041948/), and the linked [public repository landing page](https://github.com/DrubinBarnes/Akamatsu_CME_manuscript). Direct eLife and PMC page opens returned browser challenges during this review, so the article details below are grounded in their indexed primary text and exact figure/method labels. **Disposition: accept the narrowed, model-conditional prediction and the bounded next inventory step.** No experimental confirmation or general force-model discrimination follows.

Figure 7E compares Cytosim internalization across the membrane-tension sweep with a dashed **constant-work reference**, not a second simulated mechanism. The Methods define that reference using $E=\tfrac12kx^2$ and a low-load spring-resistance coefficient. Figure 1C–D defines resistance as a force–internalization slope related to membrane tension. Because $k$ and membrane tension are distinct quantities, the Methods' $0.01$ pN/nm $k$ and Fig. 7's $0.015$ pN/nm low-tension label do not establish a discrepancy without the conversion. The corrected source note makes this distinction. A flexible prescribed-force trajectory is not ruled out by the dashed curve; only the specified constant-work comparison is tested there.

The joint network prediction is supported **within the simulations**: at higher tension Fig. 7E retains more internalization than the constant-work curve, while Fig. 7F–H report more base-proximal barbed ends, more Hip1R-bound filaments, and greater bending energy. Fig. 7 supplement 1A adds bound Arp2/3 recruitment. The 7.5-nm base/neck cutoff is a model analysis definition, not an optical observable. The proposed co-variation of measured displacement and a spatially localized actin-network proxy under controlled tension is our prospective test synthesis, not a published paired experiment. The source pass does not verify a tension-conditioned experimental counterpart to the Fig. 7 network curves or a measured nanometre displacement matched to Fig. 7E; baseline molecule counting and cryo-electron tomography cannot be substituted for that joint measurement. Total actin fluorescence is also insufficient to infer barbed-end count, local bending energy, or force.

The most defensible next action is a **small, read-only README/file-inventory check** in the article-linked public repository to locate the tension-to-spring-resistance mapping, the Fig. 7 held-fixed simulation parameters, and any isolatable analysis inputs or tabular outputs. The landing page verifies a public code repository and top-level `cytosim`, `fluorescence_quantification`, and `ImageAnalysisPipeline` directories, but does not verify a reproducible Fig. 7 dataset. An eventual empirical comparison would require a separate contract for calibrated tension/load, time-resolved pit displacement, base/neck-localized network measurement, condition alignment, and independent sampling. No archive, movie, code run, force estimate, or additional empirical claim is warranted by this review.
