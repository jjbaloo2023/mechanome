# Public paired shape/coat screen

The bounded screen found one new public Source Data XLSX listing for [Nawara et al. (2022)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-022-29317-1). The experiment pairs coat intensity with a calibrated axial-distribution proxy derived from the same dual-tagged clathrin probe. Membrane-shape interpretation is indirect. Workbook contents, event IDs and biological grouping remain unverified; this is not yet a reusable event dataset.

The source and independent reviewer agree on the exact missing contract. The lead accepts the reviewer correction: the registered design allowed optical proxies and did not require a separate membrane label. Lack of that label is a limitation, not a newly imposed exclusion criterion. The decisive reuse gap is the uninspected workbook.

The source task is closed after two searches and three team primary opens. No raw data, workbook payload, fit, tracking or biological test occurred. The source's already-observed output provided link label 141 but no filename or target URL. The [source note](PAIRED_SHAPE_PUBLIC_SOURCE.md), [review](PAIRED_SHAPE_PUBLIC_REVIEW.md), [registration](paired_shape_public_design.json) and [call ledger](paired_shape_public_calls.json) preserve that boundary.

## Lead decision

A separately registered, small schema inventory is informative because a concrete public processed-object listing now exists. Its narrower question is whether the workbook exposes paired coat-intensity/axial-proxy time series, event IDs and cell/repeat grouping. It cannot independently validate membrane shape, infer force or establish scission. This is preferable to another broad search or a biological fit before the observation contract is known.

One publisher-page retrieval to identify the listed workbook and one size-limited workbook retrieval are allowed under the new registration. Stop at a schema decision or the first access/size/format failure; no retry, alternate endpoint, raw movie or analysis. The original source-screen limits remain unchanged.
