# Independent review of the queued nonlinear numerical design

**Decision: accepted after contract and formulation checks.** A bounded numerical realization can test the exact local branch from the accepted existence theorem, but sampled amplitudes are not a certified smallness threshold. Numerical convergence and positive sampled slope margins would support the designated finite cases only; they would not establish stability, global uniqueness, source recovery precision, or a biological mechanism.

Use the normalized slope `u=p/sqrt(1+p^2)=r*v`, `t=div(u*e_r)-c=2*v+r*v'-c`, and `c=epsilon*phi`. The exact zero-load flux is `Q=-(1-u^2)*t' - t*u*u' + (t^2/2+sigma)*u`. Divide `Q=0` by `r` for `r>0` to obtain

`-(1-r^2*v^2)*(v''+3*v'/r-c'/r) - t*v*(v+r*v') + (t^2/2+sigma)*v = 0`.

This preserves the complete curvature square and exact graph geometry. Radial regularity requires `v'(0)=0`; the apparent poles extend continuously because `v'/r -> v''(0)` and `c'/r -> c''(0)=-2*epsilon`. Thus the first bracket tends to `4*v''(0)-c''(0)`. The graph domain is `|r*v|<1` at every solved and evaluated radius; otherwise `p=u/sqrt(1-u^2)` ceases to be finite. The exterior source is zero for `r>=1`, yet the response slope at `r=1` is generally nonzero.

The proposed boundary condition `v(R)=0` at finite `R` approximates the flat reservoir. The `R=8` versus `R=12` comparison is needed to detect truncation bias on `[0,1]`; agreement between tolerances at one `R` cannot do so. Record the actual adaptive mesh and residual estimates for each tolerance, and inspect the exact **undivided** `Q` on an independent off-mesh grid, including radii near zero and the source edge. Report a scale-normalized residual and the largest absolute residual so a tiny amplitude cannot make a poor relative solve look good. The original graph flux reconstructed from `p`, `J`, and `C` should be checked as a separate formula path. Its agreement with the divided equation is a transcription check, not an independent equilibrium measurement.

The zero-source case should recover `v=0` under the same boundary and residual machinery. Compare the small-amplitude profile with an independently evaluated infinite-domain linear Green response for `(-Delta_4+sigma)z=-phi'/r`, `z=u/r/epsilon`; the positive heat or Bessel kernel supplies this reference. Nonlinear `u/epsilon` need not equal the linear curve at finite `epsilon` (the branch difference is order `epsilon^2`), so this is a trend and normalization check, not a zero-error acceptance criterion. Continue from the zero branch to the three preregistered amplitudes and record any branch or solver failure without declaring a contradiction of the local theorem.

For each converged configuration, report the minimum of `u_2/r` and `(u_1-u_2)/r` over `[0,1]`, with the origin limit and `r=1` included, and repeat the search on a refined independent evaluation grid. Also report `max |u|` over the full computational domain. A positive sampled margin and consistency under boundary, mesh and tolerance refinements support finite-case ordering; they are not a certified pointwise bound. If the fixed optional `g=c` probe is run only after those gates pass, compute the original undivided compatibility residual `A(rg)'+rD(cg+g^2/2)` with its amplitude scaling and no inverse or noise inference. Do not adapt `g` after viewing the profiles.

## Check of the companion formulation and draft contract

[The formulation](NONLINEAR_NUMERICAL_FORMULATION.md) agrees with the equation above. Its first-order system, finite pole condition, and Bessel Green integral follow by direct substitution; in particular the pole relation is `4*v''(0)=c''(0)+sigma*v0-0.5*v0*(2*v0-c0)*c0`. The Green formula has the required radial measure `s*ds` and its origin limit is finite. The proposed singular-matrix implementation must include `-3*w/r` exactly once and enforce the regularity condition consistently.

[The draft numerical contract](nonlinear_numerical_design.json) fixes a bounded 26-solve maximum, three amplitudes, two tensions, three domain/tolerance configurations, independent linear and zero-source controls, and a fixed optional `g=c` probe. These are adequate design controls for a finite-case computational check. The same-attempt precision correction is resolved in the frozen comparison definitions: relative changes use sup-norm `v=u/r` on the fixed 4097-point source-disk grid with a nonzero reference floor; absolute refinement changes `E_sigma` are in `v` units, and the sampled ordering gates compare `min(v_2)` with `10*E_2` and `min(v_1-v_2)` with `10*(E_1+E_2)`. This avoids pointwise relative slope error at the origin. Boundary residual is explicitly BC compliance, while the `R=8` to `R=12` comparison measures observed reservoir sensitivity. The thresholds are scoped separately to nonlinear, linear, and zero-source controls. I accept the queued contract; no formulation blocker remains. No numerical execution or finite-amplitude outcome is asserted in this review.
