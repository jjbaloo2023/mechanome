records how that same logical state was later reconciled. It is not a second
independently active campaign. Database status labels in old snapshots are dated
protocol state, not current process telemetry. The controlled marker evidence

the lead took ownership within the original window. Independent review required
exact frozen-state comparison and semantic PID-absence checks before execution.
Those corrections and the earlier failure remain in the record.

repair is queued. Return to the public-data evidence gap through a separately
