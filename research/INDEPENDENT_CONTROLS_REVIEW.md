## Disposition

**Accept a stopped curvature-versus-balanced-load empirical branch for these two studies, with a narrower Bucher data-contract task worth considering.** Eligibility does **not** demand Gaussian source geometry. The Gaussian pair was one preselected theoretical example. Another experiment could qualify by providing a different stated observation operator and a checked identifiability argument. These studies do not yet supply the independent spatial controls and registered profiles needed to rule out the unrestricted source equivalence `δf=-κΔδc`. Their narrower descriptive and calibration results remain useful within their own systems.

## Corrections and exact boundaries

The frozen requirements are correctly aimed at **source separation**, not at matching one arbitrary template. For an independently constrained source family, full profiles could in principle be replaced by a smaller set of *calibrated* observables with a demonstrated rank argument; categorical morphology and fluorescence tracks here do not provide one. Changing tension alone cannot defeat the source nullspace, even if its value were known. A second measured rigidity could help only with registered profile data and evidence that both spatial source fields remain fixed. Merely knowing net force also fails because the allowed traction and return load sum to zero.

Two same-attempt source-note corrections are resolved. The [Bucher note](INDEPENDENT_CONTROLS_BUCHER.md) now distinguishes live same-cell pre/post imaging from destructive normal/shock EM preparations, for which one-to-one membrane pairing is unestablished. The [Saleem note](INDEPENDENT_CONTROLS_SALEEM.md) now places the high-rigidity composition and observed clathrin/AP180 binding loss in **Fig. 5**; Fig. 2 shows TEM across osmotic conditions. Neither correction changes the eligibility decision. Bucher source invariance remains unestablished, while Saleem Fig. 5 directly shows that the changed-composition condition fails to preserve coat binding.

The next bounded **small-data** action is a schema-only inspection of the two checksum-pinned Bucher EM workbooks: list sheets, columns, condition and membrane identifiers, exclusions and units. Do not calculate a morphology contrast until the independent membrane units and condition mapping are verified. If those checks pass, a later descriptive reproduction of Fig. 7 morphology counts and projected-area summaries may be possible. It could not show that a given pit bends after AP2 plateau, that its tension was calibrated, or that curvature rather than balanced load caused the difference. The workbook contents have not yet been inspected in this review.

For a future source-separating experiment, the minimum remains registered physical membrane profiles or another explicitly ranked observable set, an independent mechanical scale and tension, plus either a spatial traction/reaction map or distinct calibrated rigidities with demonstrated curvature/traction invariance. Supply spatial pose, image-height calibration, condition/replicate mapping and measurement covariance. The GUV system could test a narrower coat-polymerization/tension model if its own observation and raw-data contract were established; its force and geometry cannot silently be relabeled as cellular pit traction.
