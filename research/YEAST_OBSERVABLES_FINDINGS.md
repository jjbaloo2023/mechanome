# A yeast genotype-level comparison to assess next

Retained after [independent scope review](YEAST_OBSERVABLES_REVIEW.md), 29 September 2026. [Manenschijn et al. (2019), eLife 44215](https://elifesciences.org/articles/44215/figures) lists public source data for protein abundance and inward motion under myosin-related genetic perturbations in budding yeast. The [source note](YEAST_OBSERVABLES_SOURCE.md) records the indexed publisher evidence and access limitations.

The screen did not verify paired event-level recruitment/motion records. It did identify a narrower candidate: Figure 6 lists an average-trajectory CSV and a median-Act1-molecule CSV for myo5/bbc1 perturbations. These are public file listings, not downloaded or validated tables. Genotype-level summaries could support a descriptive comparison even if the measurements come from different events; they cannot establish within-event covariance or individual causal rescue.

The lead selects Figure 6 over a broader survey of Figures 2/3 because its two named files target one concrete question: how actin abundance and inward-motion summaries vary together across the specified genotypes. Before calculating anything, verify table units, genotype labels, trajectory alignment/averaging, shared controls, replicate definitions and uncertainty. No force, membrane tension or mammalian mechanism follows from the current screen.
