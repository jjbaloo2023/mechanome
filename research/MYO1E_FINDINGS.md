(2026)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12956820/) supplies a useful experimental contrast: selected AP2/Dnm2 tracks with Myo1E recruitment under an osmotic intervention. It does not measure a per-event force law. ArpC3 measurements use a separate cell background; microaspiration measures surrounding-cell fluorescence. Neither supplies a simultaneous Myo1E/ArpC3/internalization measurement on one event.

Methods describe a baseline movie before solution addition, so pre/post acquisition identity must be checked. Movies, tracks and technical replicate means are not interchangeable biological replicates. These are interpretation constraints, not claims of a paper error.
