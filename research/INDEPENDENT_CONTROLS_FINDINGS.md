# Independent experimental controls: reviewed feasibility

The two preregistered studies do not yet support separating spatial coat curvature from balanced load. This is an eligibility decision for our inverse question, not a rejection of their own results. The unrestricted source ambiguity remains; no empirical fit or mechanism preference follows.

| System | Useful observed/calibrated quantities | Missing condition for source separation | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bucher 2018, cellular clathrin/AP2 | Projected EM areas/classes; live fluorescence timing; osmotic intervention | Calibrated local tension/rigidity, spatial traction/reaction, registered height profiles and established invariant sources | Stop mechanical inference; inspect two public EM tables for a descriptive condition comparison |

The Bucher metadata resolve seven CC BY 4.0 spreadsheets (7,575,710 bytes total). Normal and shock EM workbooks are only 78,130 and 72,748 bytes. Their contents were uninspected when the source-stage decision was made. Same-cell live pre/post imaging does not establish paired destructive EM preparations. Changed morphology/timing alone does not disprove invariant source fields: tension alone can change shape. The source invariance needed here remains unestablished. In Saleem Fig. 5, changed coat binding is a more direct failure of the preserved-source protocol. Its 8.9-MB supplement was not inspected under the 2-MB cap; no universal assertion of data absence is made.

## Decision and next bounded action

The source stage stops at its reviewed eligibility table. A separate registered follow-up may inspect only the two checksum-pinned EM workbooks: sheets, columns, units, exclusions, membrane identifiers and counts. It must establish sampling before any descriptive comparison, and cannot infer trajectories, calibrated force or mechanism. No further study search or model sweep is warranted by these results.

A future source-separating experiment needs calibrated profiles (or another explicitly ranked observation mapping), mechanical scale and tension, plus either spatial traction/reaction measurements OR distinct known rigidities with invariant curvature/load fields. Image scale/pose, condition/replicate maps and uncertainty are required. Matching a Gaussian template is not an eligibility requirement.
