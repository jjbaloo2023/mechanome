**Disposition: retain one yeast-specific candidate for a narrower genotype-aggregate comparison; stop short of an event-level joint analysis.** Manenschijn et al., *eLife* 2019, e44215 (DOI [10.7554/eLife.44215](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.44215)), is a relevant *S. cerevisiae* MYO3/MYO5 dosage perturbation. The indexed Figure 3 result associates myosin recruitment with Sla1 inward movement across genetic backgrounds. Myosin satisfies the requested actin-*or*-myosin recruitment criterion. The evidence does **not** establish that a myosin signal and Sla1 motion are paired on each of the same tracked endocytic events, rather than appearing as separate genotype-level summaries. It does not establish event IDs, trajectory alignment, selection rules, biological replicate hierarchy or uncertainty for a joint relationship. Lack of event pairing does not by itself rule out a declared genotype-level summary question.

The stated comparisons include WT and `myo3`/`myo5` deletion or dosage backgrounds, with haploid/diploid context. These are genetic perturbations, not a calibrated membrane load or tension intervention. Matching of strain background, fluorescent tagging and acquisition controls would need to be checked before a quantitative condition contrast. Sla1 inward displacement is an invagination proxy; it does not alone establish vesicle completion or force. Any observation contract from this candidate would be yeast-specific and cannot be transferred to mammalian clathrin mechanics without separate evidence.

The indexed official eLife figures listing names several potentially relevant public source-data entries: `elife-44215-fig1-data1-v1.csv` (trajectories, DOI suffix `.003`), `elife-44215-fig2-data1-v1.csv` (assembly rates, centroid speeds and peak numbers, `.006`), `elife-44215-fig3-data1-v1.csv` (average trajectories for Figure 3C/E/F, `.012`), and `elife-44215-supp1-v1.docx` (tables of Sla1 speeds by genotype, myosin molecule numbers and trajectory counts). Figure 6 also lists average trajectories (`elife-44215-fig6-data1-v1.csv`, `.019`) and median Act1 molecules (`elife-44215-fig6-data2-v1.csv`, `.020`) for a `myo5`/`bbc1` double-deletion contrast. These listings are stronger evidence for a possible genotype-aggregate contract than the two tiny Figure 3 supplemental Abp1-lifetime and molecule-median files alone. They do **not** verify any body, column schema, units, same-event ID, channel pairing, replicate linkage or selection rule. Direct article/figure access returned a JavaScript challenge/HTTP 403. Public *listings* are verified; usable access to these bodies and availability of underlying event-level records, including whether any are request-only, remain unresolved. This is a measurement-contract limit, **not** an assertion that joint data are absent.

The narrow next eligibility check, if separately justified, is the body/schema and relevant methods for the specifically listed Figure 2/3 aggregate files and supplement tables: genotype and control ancestry, units, trajectory averaging, selection, uncertainty and independent replicate counts. An event-level question would additionally require paired event IDs and both marker/motion fields. Until then there is no quantitative genotype contrast or same-event analysis from this screen, and no yeast-to-mammal mechanistic transfer.
