# Bucher EM package audit: unresolved count discrepancy

`check_bucher_inclusion.py` inspected the two cached, registered XLSX packages directly as ZIP/XML. Both source bytes match their registered lengths, MD5s, and prior SHA-256 values. The check executes no formulas and treats an area entry as a stored numeric cell with a finite value greater than zero.

The baseline workbook contains 735, 857, and 726 qualifying entries in its three labeled cell blocks, while the published Fig. 1 totals supplied to this audit are 746, 869, and 739. The osmotic-shock workbook contains 257, 299, 226, and 321 qualifying entries for its `Control` blocks, versus Fig. 7 totals 267, 308, 229, and 323; its `osmotic shock` blocks contain 390, 95, 347, and 200, versus 395, 99, 351, and 201. Each published total is higher than its corresponding deposited count.

All inspected numeric area cells are finite and positive. The audit validates the actual unit, condition, `Cell n`, and `Flat`/`Dome`/`Pit` headers against its declared block map, and verifies that no numeric cell occurs outside those area blocks or on the empty extra sheets. The JSON record gives every morphology column's count, first and last numeric row, compact row ranges for internal gaps, stored blank cells, and trailing padding to the longest column in its block, plus numeric/blank style counts, nonnumeric cells, and invalid numeric cells. It also records hidden row/column settings, merges, formulas, comments, defined names, document properties, style definitions, sheet protection, and package size. No formula, comment, defined name, style setting, hidden region, or document metadata in these files provides an explicit mapping from deposited entries to the higher Fig. 1 or Fig. 7 totals.

Trailing cells after a shorter list ends are ragged-column padding. A gap between a column's first and last numeric rows is reported separately as an internal blank. This structural distinction does not establish a missing-data mechanism or author inclusion rule.

The package evidence therefore does not reconcile the captions. It must not be used to alter denominators, fill values, reproduce published percentages, calculate morphology effects or p-values, or make biological or mechanical claims. The named source/supplement review is the only remaining bounded route to an explicit reconciliation; without one, figure reproduction remains stopped.
