# Public dynamic CME data: bounded discovery note

Scope: candidate sources that add dynamic within-pit trajectories or a tension/adaptor perturbation to the static geometry in BioStudies S-BIAD566.  This is a discovery note, **not** a download, reanalysis, or validation of any model.  Statuses below reflect public landing pages and primary articles checked on 2026-09-19.

2026-09-20 update: [DYNAMIC_METADATA.md](DYNAMIC_METADATA.md) records the subsequent
access audit. Shape2Fate validation contains one movie pair and three annotation
CSVs. `trajectories.csv` is generated by the example, not a deposited ZIP member.
Mendeley condition metadata remain inaccessible.

## 1. Tension-linked raw TIRF/SIM images — Liu, Wang & Gong (2023)

**Status: verified public landing page and downloadable file listing; file contents/condition labels not yet inspected.**

* **Repository / persistent locator:** Mendeley Data, DOI [10.17632/pfgfbv3rjx.1](https://doi.org/10.17632/pfgfbv3rjx.1), [record landing page](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/pfgfbv3rjx/1).  The record is CC BY 4.0 and lists a 354 MB `TIRF raw data.zip` plus 6.26 GB `SIMrawdata.zip`; the public page exposes “Download All.”
* **What it measures:** the depositor describes “clathrin coated pits images” acquired by fixed-cell SIM and live-bottom-surface TIRF.  Thus it is a plausible source for clathrin intensity/lifetime, footprint/size, and spatial-distribution trajectories after tracking; it is not a precomputed track table on the public landing page.
* **Mechanism-discriminating condition:** the dataset title is *Effect of cell membrane tension on the lifetime and size of mature clathrin-coated pits and their spatial distribution*.  It is therefore a direct candidate for comparing CCP dynamics across a membrane-tension manipulation, rather than a static-geometry-only comparison.
* **Metadata grouping:** unresolved.  The web-visible record gives authors, date, licence, two archive names, and a terse acquisition description, but not file-to-condition/cell mapping, frame interval, fluorescent label, tension intervention, or replicate identity.  Those must be read from the archive before analyses can be grouped correctly.
* **Curvature/timing interpretation:** timing is potentially recoverable from the live TIRF movie frames.  Curvature is **not direct** in conventional TIRF; SIM here is stated to be fixed-cell imaging, so any curvature conclusion would need a stated proxy or separate calibration.
* **Primary-source locator:** [Mendeley record](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/pfgfbv3rjx/1), especially its description, licence, and file list.  No matching primary-paper DOI was verified in this bounded search; do not attach this record to the 2019 modelling paper by overlapping authors without confirmation.

## 2. Event-resolved clathrin/cargo time series with stimulation — Harmanec & Kadlecova, Shape2Fate (2025)

**Status: verified public Zenodo record and file types from its record description; individual archive members not downloaded or opened.**

* **Repository / persistent locator:** Zenodo DOI [10.5281/zenodo.17484958](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17484958), [record 17484958](https://zenodo.org/records/17484958).  The companion public code is [harmanea/shape2fate](https://github.com/harmanea/shape2fate); its README identifies `trajectories.csv` as linked, filtered trajectories in the validation dataset.
* **What it measures:** raw live-cell time-lapse TIRF-SIM, usually `.dv`, with some reconstructed images, masks, and manual annotation CSVs.  Reporters named by the record are EGFP-CLCa, SNAP-CLCa, SBP-mEmerald-LAMP1, pHluorin-GLUT4, and Dynamin2-mRuby3.  This supports within-event clathrin temporal traces and, in appropriate acquisitions, temporal association with cargo/exocytic and dynamin signals.
* **Mechanism-discriminating conditions:** `RUSH` datasets labelled `_STIM` were biotin stimulated; adipocyte GLUT4 `_STIM` datasets were insulin stimulated.  These are explicit, externally timed perturbations that can test event timing/recruitment changes.  They do **not** supply a membrane-tension intervention or an actin reporter.
* **Metadata grouping:** partial.  The record says raw-file acquisition parameters are stored in metadata and identifies reporter and stimulus conventions.  Exact cell line, replicate, channel pairing, acquisition cadence, and which archives include annotations remain unresolved until the member manifest/metadata is inspected.
* **Curvature/timing interpretation:** timing is direct at the imaging-frame level; spatial morphology comes from super-resolution TIRF-SIM.  Axial membrane curvature is **not directly measured** by this modality alone, so ring/footprint shape must not be converted into curvature without an independently justified calibration.
* **Primary-source locator:** [Zenodo record description](https://zenodo.org/records/17484958); the record links the associated Shape2Fate manuscript/preprint.  This is a recent dataset and should be citation-checked against the final paper before reuse.

## 3. Large raw CCP dynamics deposit including adaptor/dynamin perturbation context — Wang et al., DASC (2020)

**Status: public dataset citation and DOI verified through the primary eLife article and cmeAnalysis repository; direct Figshare member listing was not retrievable in this session.**

* **Repository / persistent locator:** Figshare DOI [10.35092/yhjc.12198225](https://doi.org/10.35092/yhjc.12198225), titled *Live-cell imaging data for DASC (disassembly asymmetry score classification) analysis of clathrin-mediated endocytosis*.  The public [cmeAnalysis repository README](https://github.com/DanuserLab/cmeAnalysis/blob/master/README.md) links the deposit as a 208 GB DASC dataset, and the eLife primary article’s [Data availability section](https://elifesciences.org/articles/53686) identifies it as raw data.
* **What it measures:** live fluorescence CCP movies intended for cmeAnalysis/DASC detection, tracking, intensity traces, lifetime analysis, and classification of clathrin-coat assembly/disassembly.  cmeAnalysis explicitly supports master/slave multichannel tracking; the associated 2013 checkpoint paper is the relevant methodological context for AP2 and dynamin trajectories.
* **Mechanism-discriminating conditions:** the DASC primary study tests separability of early clathrin coat assembly/disassembly processes and reports condition-dependent analyses; the public dataset title alone does not verify a file-level map for cargo, adaptor, dynamin, knockdown, or drug groups.  Treat those groups as **unresolved**, not assumed.
* **Metadata grouping:** unresolved until the Figshare manifest/readme is viewed.  The verified facts are the persistent DOI, raw-data designation, and approximate total size.  Replicate/cell/condition identifiers, fluorophores, frame rate, channels, and analysis-ready track files need a small manifest check before selection.
* **Curvature/timing interpretation:** event timing and fluorescence trajectories are direct observables after tracking.  Curvature is **inferred at best** from fluorescence morphology/intensity unless a specific DASC subdataset contains an independent axial or polarization readout; no such readout was verified here.
* **Primary-source locator:** Wang X. et al., *DASC, a sensitive classifier for measuring discrete early stages in clathrin-mediated endocytosis*, eLife 2020, [article 53686](https://elifesciences.org/articles/53686), which names this DOI in Data availability.

## Explicit exclusions and use boundary

* Scott et al. 2018, [*Membrane bending occurs at all stages of clathrin-coat assembly and defines endocytic dynamics*](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-02818-8), is valuable because pol-TIRF directly reports membrane orientation/curvature dynamics, but its raw data were reported as available on request.  It is **not counted** as a public raw dataset here.
* Willy et al. 2021, [*De novo Endocytic Clathrin Coats Develop Curvature at Early Stages of Their Formation*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.devcel.2021.10.019), has public supplementary movies but reports microscopy data available upon request.  Those movies are useful qualitative exemplars, not a replacement public raw trajectory dataset.
* The static S-BIAD566 geometry collection remains complementary: it provides high-resolution fixed-state geometries and pseudo-time, whereas the three candidates above can add real movie time and/or an explicit perturbation.  None alone establishes that geometry and time arise from the same individual pit.
