# Independent review: Manenschijn Figure 6 two-file contract

Task `yeast-figure6-review-001`, 2026-09-29. Reviewed the registered [design](yeast_figure6_contract_design.json), the [source contract](YEAST_FIGURE6_SOURCE.md), [request ledger](metadata/yeast-figure6-001/REQUEST_LEDGER.md), both saved publisher CSVs and the lead's retained indexed-primary Figure 6 caption/methods locators. I made no remote request, author-code call or numerical genotype contrast.

**Disposition: accept a partial, figure-level genotype-aggregate observation contract; withhold an inferential or event-level comparison.** The exact [Figure 6B Sla1 CSV](metadata/yeast-figure6-001/01-elife-44215-fig6-data1-v1.csv) and [Figure 6A Act1 CSV](metadata/yeast-figure6-001/02-elife-44215-fig6-data2-v1.csv) were retrieved from the named eLife CDN paths with HTTP 200, `text/csv`, 39,769 and 210 bytes respectively. Their independently checked SHA-256 hashes match the ledger (`bc92afb9...ffbc174f70866fa` and `bf468206...bda9c4ba38d0`); total downloaded body was 39,979 bytes. The team used its two registered primary queries, and the reviewer used zero.

The Act1 file has four aggregate rows, with `Protein`, `genotype`, `median nr of molecules`, and `SEM`: WT, `myo5del`, `bbc1del`, and the double deletion. It contains no underlying molecule observations, sample count, biological-unit IDs or definition of the SEM's sampling unit, so the caption's z-tests cannot be reconstructed from this table alone. The Sla1 file has four genotype blocks with `t (s)`, `x (nm)`, `n`, and `x_err (nm)`. These are average centroid-position trajectories, not already estimated inward speeds, scission times or force. The double mutant appears as `myo5delbbc1del` in Sla1 and `bbc1delmyo5del` in Act1; joining by row order would be wrong, while mapping by the two deleted genes is a plausible genotype-key normalization, not evidence of common cells or experiments.

The Figure 6 caption describes plotted inward-phase Sla1 movement with shaded 95% confidence intervals, but the **full CSV includes negative times** (WT begins at -18.063 s), while the `bbc1del` block begins at positive 15.4215 s. The saved table does not define time zero, alignment, trajectory averaging or the exact plotted subset; direct same-time comparisons across blocks are therefore unjustified. `x_err` is an error column associated with the captioned interval; its exact conversion to a CI half-width and independent biological unit are not established by the CSV. `n` varies by time point; an independent parse found minimum `n=3` and **no zero `n` rows**. It must not be called an independent biological-replicate count. Time rows are correlated samples of aggregate trajectories, not statistical replicates. A later descriptive motion functional would need a declared reference for `x`, common observable range and alignment interpretation before calculation; it would not reproduce a published speed without the actual method.

The indexed caption says WT and `myo5del` Act1 values reuse Figure 1 data, while WT and `myo5del` Sla1 trajectories reuse Figure 2 data. Those are shared controls within each readout, not fresh validation or independent replication. The two files do not link Act1 and Sla1 within events, cells or culture replicates; any cross-readout comparison is of genotype summaries only. Strain/tagging control ancestry and selection/censoring rules are still needed to interpret those summaries across genotypes. Sla1 centroid motion is an invagination proxy and the Act1 measure is abundance; neither is calibrated load, force, vesicle completion or a transferable mammalian mechanism.

A separately registered, small *descriptive* calculation could report the four published Act1 medians/SEMs beside a prespecified Sla1 trajectory summary, clearly marked as aggregate and with shared controls. The present files alone do not support a joint event covariance, a new uncertainty test across genotypes, or a causal rescue claim. The remaining exact gaps are trajectory alignment/plotted-window definition, source of `x_err` and `n`, biological replication and selection, and matched strain/cell ancestry across readouts. No additional figures or data are required to state this limited contract.
