# Independent endpoints: a paper-level candidate, no reanalysis yet

Reviewed on 29 September 2026. The bounded screen found a relevant same-event
assay, but did not verify a public event-level data object. No new tracker score,
biological effect or model fit was calculated.

[Mattheyses, Atkinson and Simon (2011)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3245319/)
paired clathrin-dsRed with TfR-pHluorin at selected puncta. Alternating external
pH probes loss of proton accessibility separately from clathrin intensity
decline. The paper distinguishes that assay from full topological scission,
allows hemi-scission, and notes that sealing during low pH is not detected.
Figure 3 retained 80 of 90 selected puncta after excluding 10 that regained
pH sensitivity. Its categories concern the onset of clathrin decline and
movie-end censoring, not automated track termination.

Those selected observations cannot supply all-event tracker sensitivity or
predictive validity. We did not verify a public per-event table/movie identity,
assay times, exclusions or biological grouping. That is an access/contract gap,
not proof the data are absent. The [source record](ENDPOINT_PUBLIC_SOURCE.md)
and [review](ENDPOINT_PUBLIC_REVIEW.md) retain exact locators and limitations.

This narrows the next-data requirement: an independent same-event endpoint
could validate a track-end proxy without measuring membrane forces. It still
needs a validated assay, temporal association/lag and censoring rules, eligibility
and cell/preparation grouping. A dynamin flash or the disappearance being tested
cannot serve as its own independent reference.

The screen stops here. It used two source searches, one source primary open and
one lead verification open, below the two-query/three-open caps; zero local
downloads, code execution, fits or tracking. The lead corrected an initial
Merrifield attribution and an onset-versus-termination ambiguity before
acceptance. No additional access rescue is queued.

The next task addresses the autonomous-operation demonstration: a separately
registered offline-controller work-unit budget extension with dummy calls and
restart checks. It must preserve money/attempt semantics and historical evidence.
It will not enforce live agent limits until execution paths actually use it.
