# No distinct actin-load experiment is supported by the assessed inputs

Task `campaign-next-experiment-001`, 29 September 2026.  The assessed record
does **not** support a distinct next experiment for the actin-load comparison.
The only concrete joint load/network/internalization contrast is already the accepted
`ACTIN_ADAPTATION_FINDINGS` prospective candidate; its separate bounded
partial code-metadata inventory is also completed and closed.  Restating either as a new branch would
be duplication, not a new falsifiable route.
This note records the exact new-data prerequisite needed before that existing
candidate can become an experiment.  It is not a fit, public-data reanalysis,
or evidence that the required matched measurement already exists.

## Existing candidate and its exact boundary

The already accepted prospective candidate would, under predeclared low and
high independently calibrated membrane-load conditions, measure each pit's
membrane-normal internalization displacement and a base/neck-localized
actin-network proxy at matched coat stage.  Its comparison is intentionally
narrow:

| Frozen reference | Network-adaptation prediction from the reviewed simulation comparison | Discriminating result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An independent force-displacement/resistance calibration would set and freeze `x0`, `k0` and the low-load constant-work relation `E = 1/2 k x^2`; no high-load curve fitting is allowed.  A curve such as `x(k)=x0 sqrt(k0/k)` is unavailable without that calibration. | In the Akamatsu Figure 7 simulation, higher-load sweep summaries retain more internalization than this constant-work reference, while the network summaries report more base-proximal barbed ends, Hip1R-bound filaments and bending energy.  These summaries do not establish same-event covariance. | If a calibrated proxy link, measurement power and selection contract are established, high-load displacement above the frozen reference **and** a proxy rise at matched coat stage/age/duration supports the stated empirical proxy prediction.  A failed or unvalidated proxy measurement is unresolved, not a refutation of simulated barbed ends or bending energy. |

The dashed reference is neither a separately simulated non-adaptive mechanism
nor a general non-actin mechanism.  The network proxy is not a direct barbed-
end, bending-energy or force measurement.  Therefore an outcome
would test the stated adaptive-network versus constant-work comparison only; it
would not identify every possible prescribed-force history or a molecular cause.

## Observable and analysis contract

The primary unit is a pit nested within a cell and independently repeated
biological preparation, with cells and preparations retained rather than pooled
as independent pits.  For every accepted event, retain:

1. Calibrated membrane-normal displacement relative to a registered membrane or
   surface reference, with its time coordinate and coat-stage alignment.
2. A feasible measured proxy: predeclared two-color base/neck-ROI F-actin
   reporter signal after local background correction, in the same event and
   time bin.  Its ROI, registration, reporter response and selection limits
   must be calibrated before condition labels are scored; it cannot be renamed
   a 7.5-nm barbed-end count, Hip1R-bound-filament count or bending energy.
3. Total event/presence coverage and eligible-only coverage for each condition,
   plus the observation exclusions and track supports.  Total fluorescence alone
   is not the local proxy; post-tracking intensity rescaling cannot repair a
   condition-specific missed-event process.

Compare high versus low load on a frozen joint score: deviation of displacement
from the frozen reference and the direction of the localized proxy at matched
coat stage, event age and duration stratum.  Predeclare how censored or
unmatched-duration events are retained or reported.  Report all events and all
prespecified exclusions; otherwise slower or longer-lived events alone could
create apparent proxy enrichment.  Do
not substitute a static cap angle/area table for displacement or a population
geometry ranking for a single-pit time course.

## Controls required before the observation is informative

* Establish an independent force-displacement/resistance calibration that fixes
  `x0`, `k0` and the manipulation-to-resistance/tension mapping.  Do not equate
  a reported membrane-tension number or a low-load displacement alone with the
  simulation spring coefficient `k`.
* Calibrate the displacement reference and the local-network imaging response
  across load conditions, including axial registration, background, bleaching,
  label expression and point-spread changes.
* Freeze the independently calibrated `x0`, `k0` and any displacement curve
  before examining high-load events.  Hold coat-stage alignment, event-age and
  duration handling, imaging settings and the localization ROI fixed.
* Validate the reporter-to-proxy link, its detection power and condition-specific
  selection response before interpreting a null proxy effect.  Include
  acquisition/date, cell and biological-preparation identifiers; assign
  train/control versus held-out biological replicates before any threshold or
  normalization choice.  Track counts and local measurement quality must be
  reported by condition so a change in selection is not reported as adaptation.
* Retain a no-load-change imaging control and a localization/registration
  control.  These check the observation path; they do not themselves establish
  a force or network mechanism.

## Current availability and decision

The campaign has no verified input containing all three elements—calibrated
load, time-resolved displacement, and a base/neck-localized network measurement
for the same events.  The reviewed Akamatsu source supports the **simulation**
comparison and a public repository landing page, but not a tension-conditioned,
experimentally matched Figure 7 table.  S-BIAD566 supplies static cap fits, and
DASC/Shape2Fate retain narrower observation contracts; neither supplies this
joint observable.  The geometry-transfer result is descriptive and unstable
across line/policy, while the known-load result requires complete profiles and a
known spatial load, so neither is an alternative test of this prediction.

Thus the immediate result is an exact prerequisite for **this load-adaptation
comparison**, not a new follow-up: assessed inputs cannot discriminate it until
new data provide the
same-event paired observable with an independently calibrated load-to-resistance
mapping, calibrated displacement reference, localized-reporter contract,
condition alignment and independent culture/cell units.  The partial repository
metadata inventory is completed and must not be repackaged here as a new task.
If those data do not exist, stop and record the missing measurement; do not
clone, run Cytosim, download movies, or infer it from baseline fluorescence.

## Value, budget and stop condition

No distinct test clears the existing-evidence gate **within the presently
assessed inputs**.  A purported independent endpoint-versus-fluorescence-
disappearance route does not: DASC's first
detection, validity selection and disappearance are observation-defined, and
the assessed Shape2Fate contracts provide neither a physical endpoint nor the
paired load/network measurements.  Recasting either as scission or force would
repeat the known observation gap.  Another cap-model fit or dimming simulation
would likewise add less than the specified new-data prerequisite.  This decision
does not reopen the stopped yeast, Myo1E, Bucher, Akamatsu-reproduction,
nonlinear-BVP, duration-null or unknown-load branches.

A genuinely new dataset with an independently assay-defined sealing or
internalization endpoint, linked to each clathrin track with known temporal lag,
assay sensitivity/specificity, censoring and biological grouping, could support
a distinct **measurement-validation** question.  It would test whether track
disappearance is an adequate endpoint proxy; it would not establish scission
without topology validation and would not require the load/network controls of
the separate actin comparison.  No such dataset is verified here.

No execution is authorized by this synthesis.  Any future inventory, new-data
screen or observation must have its own registration and sampling/calibration
budget.  Only new paired data plus a separately specified plan can justify an
actin observation execution registration.
