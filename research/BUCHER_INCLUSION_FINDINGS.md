# Bucher EM inclusion check: reproduction stopped

The bounded reconciliation did not explain the difference between deposited
area-entry totals and the Fig. 1/Fig. 7 caption counts. Stop published-figure
reproduction for these workbooks. This is an unresolved data-contract issue,
not evidence that the publication is wrong or that a biological effect is absent.

The [package audit](BUCHER_INCLUSION_PACKAGE.md) verified both source hashes and
read actual numeric cells. All 4,453 deposited area values are finite and positive;
there are no internal column gaps. No inspected hidden regions, comments, names,
formulas or metadata explain the differences. Normal ragged-column padding does
not identify missing structures. The baseline has three labeled cell blocks;
the shock workbook contains both four-cell control and four-cell shock arms.
Neither reused cell labels nor distinct membrane labels establishes paired
pre/post samples or independent experimental cultures.

The [source audit](BUCHER_INCLUSION_SOURCE.md) inspected the primary article and
one 972,260-byte supplement. Its explicit filters concern fluorescence tracks
and model histograms; they do not supply an EM entry-to-caption mapping. No
culture/day or matched-membrane map was established. See the [primary article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-018-03533-0)
and [supplement](https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41467-018-03533-0/MediaObjects/41467_2018_3533_MOESM1_ESM.pdf)
for the authors' own results and methods.

The [independent review](BUCHER_INCLUSION_REVIEW.md) accepts the unresolved
outcome. Do not impute missing categories or use caption denominators for
sensitivity bounds without first establishing that deposited entries form the
claimed subset. No effect, p-value, trajectory or mechanical fit was calculated.
A future description of deposited entries would require its own declared scope
and unknown-selection caveat; it would not reproduce the published figure.

Further source retries are not warranted without changed inputs. The campaign
can instead test an explicit boundary of its mechanics result: the known
curvature/load ambiguity was proved for a shallow linear membrane. A separate
nonlinear analysis must freeze source coordinates, work/reaction and boundary
conditions, and cannot create empirical permission for these datasets.
