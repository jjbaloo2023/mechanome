# Independent review: same-event endpoint source screen

Task `endpoint-public-review-001`, 29 September 2026. I checked the registered [screen](endpoint_public_design.json), the corrected [source note](ENDPOINT_PUBLIC_SOURCE.md), the existing DASC observation boundary, and the lead's recorded primary-page cross-check. I made no remote requests, downloads, fits, tracking calls or BVP solves.

**Disposition: accept one paper-level feasibility candidate, but no public track-level reanalysis contract.** The bounded search identified Mattheyses, Atkinson and Simon (2011), *Traffic*, PMC3245319. In Figure 3, TfR-pHluorin and clathrin-dsRed are observed at the same selected puncta while external pH cycles. The loss of TfR-pHluorin response is an assay-defined loss of extracellular proton accessibility, independent of clathrin fluorescence decline. This is a genuinely different observation question from force recovery or another analysis of DASC/Shape2Fate tracks. It does not require calibrated spatial load.

The source now correctly distinguishes accessibility/sealing from complete topological scission. The paper itself notes possible hemi-scission, and sealing in the low-pH phase is not detectable. Of 90 selected colocalized assay-positive puncta, 10 regained pH sensitivity and were excluded; the Figure 3 proportions apply to the retained 80. The reported 22.5% near sealing, 50% delayed and 27.5% unresolved concern **onset of clathrin intensity decline**, not an automated track-termination rule. The unresolved group is censored at movie end. These selected-case categories cannot estimate sensitivity, specificity, PPV, NPV, or all-pit event coverage for a tracker. Co-localization, pH phase and exclusions are part of the selection process; cell, movie and preparation IDs were not recovered in this pass.

The bounded primary inspection verified no named public per-event table, movie object, accession or request-only data term. This is **unverified data identity/access**, not evidence that the data are absent. The paper's Figure 3 percentages alone do not allow a new per-event analysis. A future *separately registered* availability check would need an actual public object and event IDs linking clathrin traces, pH phase and assay endpoint times, re-sensitizing exclusions, censoring, and cell/movie/preparation grouping. A prospective tracker-validation contrast would then require a frozen track-termination definition, time-lag window and assay controls. Do not relabel either dynamin signal or fluorescence disappearance as the endpoint, or convert this assay into a force or biological-mechanism result.

The initial source draft misattributed the paper to Merrifield and blurred clathrin decline with track termination. The lead's same-primary check and source correction resolved both before this acceptance. The call ledger reports two source searches, one source primary open and one lead primary open within the team limits; no data acquisition followed. Stop this screen at the paper-level candidate and exact public-data/grouping gap.
